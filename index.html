🚀 Our Mission & Purpose

Bus Yatra (operated by Bus Yatra Passenger Mobility Solutions) is an integrated digital transit enterprise. We bridge the critical connectivity divide between growing sub-divisional towns—such as Pingla, Debra, Kharagpur, and Midnapore—and the metropolitan hub of Kolkata (Esplanade & Babughat).

Our platform empowers commuters to locate scheduled services, reserve seats transparently, track bus locations via live telemetry, and receive verified digital passes directly on their smartphone with zero counter waiting time.

Terms and Conditions of Passenger Carriage

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Valid for all digital passes and services

1. Digital Ticketing & Boarding Requirement

Every passenger must possess a valid Bus Yatra digital pass with a verified Booking ID and dynamic QR code. Passengers are required to present the digital pass (or printout) along with a Government-recognized photo identification card (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, Passport, or Driving License) upon boarding.

2. Reporting & Boarding Discipline

Passengers must arrive at their designated boarding stage at least 10 minutes prior to scheduled departure. Buses adhere to strict stage-dispatch schedules. Bus Yatra and partner operators bear no liability for passengers who arrive late (classified as a No-Show).

3. Seat Occupancy & Allocation

Passengers are assigned numbered seats upon booking confirmation and must occupy only their designated seat. Seat reassignments without the bus captain's express authorization are prohibited.

4. Luggage & Baggage Guidelines

Each passenger is entitled to carry up to 15 kg of personal luggage free of charge. Hazardous, combustible, explosive, flammable, toxic, offensive-smelling, or contraband materials prohibited under Indian law (including the Motor Vehicles Act) are strictly prohibited onboard.

5. Code of Passenger Conduct

Smoking, consumption of alcohol or narcotics, and disruptive behavioral conduct are strictly forbidden onboard. Any verbal or physical harassment of fellow passengers or crew members will result in immediate removal from the coach and reporting to law enforcement authorities.

6. Platform Role & Limitation of Liability

Bus Yatra acts as an authorized technology aggregator connecting commuters with certified local bus operators. While we enforce stringent vehicle health and punctuality covenants, unforeseen highway disruptions, floods, strikes, or sudden police diversions are governed under force majeure principles.

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Standards

In compliance with Indian IT Act 2000, SPDI Rules 2011, and Google Publisher Policies

1. Scope & Commitment to Privacy

Bus Yatra respects and protects your digital privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, process, safeguard, and retain user information across our web portal, Progressive Web App (PWA), and digital ticketing interfaces.

2. Personal Information Collected

We collect personal information necessary to deliver transit services, specifically: Full Name, 10-digit Mobile Number, Gender, Age, Boarding Stage, Dropping Stage, Payment Reference ID, temporary device GPS location (accessed strictly upon user consent when viewing live bus tracking or triggering SOS), and optional SOS emergency live camera frames (captured solely when explicitly permitted by the user during an active SOS alert and automatically wiped upon resolution or stop).

3. Use of Personal Information

Information collected is utilized exclusively to:

  • Generate, deliver, and cryptographically verify digital bus tickets.
  • Send travel updates, gate notifications, and departure reminders.
  • Calculate real-time bus distance and dynamic passenger pickup ETA.
  • Provide passenger manifests to authorized conductors for safety.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce access control, and comply with state transport regulations.
4. Zero Data Selling Guarantee

We maintain an unequivocal policy: Bus Yatra does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal data to any third-party advertisers, marketing firms, or data brokers. Data is shared only with verified trip operators and encrypted cloud infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Google Cloud Firebase).

5. Security, Cookies & Google Standards

All network communications are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption. We utilize local storage strictly for maintaining user sessions, language preferences, and offline PWA reliability in accordance with Google Ads and Webmaster standards.

6. Data Rights & Deletion

Users have the statutory right to request access to their booking records or request permanent deletion of their customer account by sending an email to support.busyatra@gmail.com.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Bus Operator-Driven Cancellation Policy & Direct Owner Helpline Support

রিফান্ড ও টিকিট বাতিল সরাসরি বাস মালিকের নিয়মানুযায়ী সম্পন্ন হয়

Bus Yatra একটি ডিজিটাল টিকেট বুকিং প্ল্যাটফর্ম। যাত্রী যে নির্দিষ্ট বাসের টিকিট বুক করেছেন, সেই বাসের মালিক ও কর্তৃপক্ষের নিজস্ব নিয়মানুযায়ী এবং তাদের সিদ্ধান্তের ওপর রিফান্ড বা ট্রিপ পরিবর্তন সম্পূর্ণরূপে নির্ভরশীল।

👉 টিকিট বাতিল বা রিফান্ডের জন্য যাত্রীকে অবশ্যই তার ডিজিটাল টিকিটে উল্লেখিত নির্দিষ্ট বাস মালিক / কন্ডাক্টরের হেল্পলাইন নম্বরে সরাসরি কল করতে হবে।

1. Operator-Discretionary Cancellation & Refund Framework

All ticket cancellations, seat reassignments, and refund requests depend strictly on the individual bus owner / fleet operator operating the scheduled journey. Because different private bus operators have distinct operating schedules and stage routes, each bus operator enforces their own cancellation timeframe and refund rules.

2. How to Request Cancellation & Refund

To cancel a booking or claim a refund, passengers must directly call the respective Bus Owner / Conductor Helpline Number printed on their digital ticket pass, SMS confirmation, or booking receipt. The bus operator will verify your Booking ID, travel date, and seat number to assist you with the cancellation or rescheduling.

3. Trip Cancellation or Mechanical Breakdown

In the event a scheduled trip is cancelled by the respective bus operator due to unforeseen mechanical breakdown or road hurdles and no alternate transport is provided, passengers should immediately contact the bus owner's helpline to receive their full refund as per operator terms.

4. Platform Assistance & Central Escalation

If you face any difficulty contacting your assigned bus operator or need help obtaining the operator's direct phone number, please contact Bus Yatra central support at +91 87684 47312 or email support.busyatra@gmail.com with your Booking ID.
